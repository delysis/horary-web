## Separate book and authored-gold questions

The Information explicit/implicit cases preserve the claim and method but use kind=property rather than gold's kind=other. The contract permits property, and pp164–165 do not impose this internal enum. That mismatch appears over-specific; the original grades remain untouched. Their misleading-progress replies remain separate defects.

The school-level contract misses a source distinction: elementary uses radical third, anything above elementary radical ninth (pp219–220), including secondary education. Its current school/university enum should not silently assign secondary school to third. The independently authored proposal contains additive branch cases and source projections; no frozen bank was altered.

The family capacity schema omits cousin although printed p19 explicitly places cousins in third. A natural-language cousin should not be replaced by stranger or turned into an unnecessary relationship interview. The additive proposal preserves literal cousin and separately tests inferred cousin from two mothers being sisters.

Direct questions about a daughter marrying and genuine relays of her own question need distinct actor frames (pp137–138, 26–27). The external proposal records the affected person separately from the questioner and unnamed prospective partner. Correct receipt structure does not qualify a still-unimplemented turned-role recipe.

## Integrated repairs and remaining qualification

A narrow affirmative ownership repair preserves the quoted evidence while rejecting negation, hypotheses and reported first-person speech. Native device-anchor handling excludes complete phrases such as “here, now” and “right here” while retaining literal named places. Geocoding preserves city-name words and only reads trailing state qualifiers, including New York City/NYC, Kansas City and Texas City. The original DST scenarios had stalled before their moment checks because of this city normalization defect. “How much does my husband love me?” also exposed an overbroad numerical-prefix guard; it now permits the qualitative relationship question while retaining unambiguous count protection.
