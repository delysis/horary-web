# Reviewing Horary with Eileen

Start with the [executable reading catalogue](READING_CONTRACTS.md). Its 44 source-cited contracts specify the facts each kind of question needs, conditional questions, allowed answers, role rules and judgment boundaries. The Rust catalogue tracks required facts and decides whether a reading may begin. A separate conversational model speaks to you, supported by those private reminders; catalogue questions are examples for it, not a response script. Classification can change while already supplied facts remain. Sixteen programs have a wired worksheet path; 28 specialist programs collect their specified inputs but wait for expert review before interpretation. Those counts describe implementation coverage, not astrologically qualified answers.

The [living process map](LLM_PROCESS.md) traces every model/tool stage, place and time selection, and where interpretation appears. Its task table distinguishes classification, horary reasoning and native calculation. Exact model instructions, generated schemas and authored prompt examples are linked alongside the diagrams. Normal Rust tests check both references against the application source. The [verification record](VERIFICATION.md) separates controller tests, actual Gemma probes and outstanding interpretation/voice assessment.

Open the native Horary app and tell it what you want to know. It speaks and listens, with text entry only when microphone access or capture is unavailable. There is no settings screen or chart form. The first conversation prepares the local model; an empty machine downloads roughly 7.15 GB into the shared Hugging Face cache, without Python, a CLI, an account, or a duplicate app copy.

At launch, the app requests microphone, speech recognition and location access, then obtains the device’s location and clock context. If location access is unavailable, the reader asks in conversation. A denied microphone reveals a quiet writing line and an icon for microphone access. Returning from System Settings refreshes authorization; changing microphone consent may require relaunch. Each launch checks again without storing refusal as a preference. An explicitly supplied reader location takes priority. Bob's market venue is kept separately and does not override where the question is considered. The app should ask natural questions when it needs context. The chart and the reading appear on a scroll as the conversation develops. Ask “Why did you choose that house?”, “What does the book say?”, or “What would change if this belonged to my sister?” Say corrections aloud; earlier spoken passages remain readable and preserved.

On macOS, answer after the document speaks, or address it with “Oracle” when it is waiting. For example: “Oracle, where is my ring?” It lights up while hearing the question and finishes after a pause. The luminous “?” also starts manual capture on a press and finishes it on another, including when wake listening is unavailable. While working, pressing it pauses the reply. Installed on-device dictation supplies words when available; otherwise the manual route sends the recording directly to the local reader. Direct understanding appears as “From your spoken words,” a summary you can correct aloud. macOS shows separate permission dialogs; the app requests them together in its startup sequence. Spoken replies use its installed voice. Escape pauses work or listening without discarding completed passages. Wake listening stops when the window is inactive; it never sends ambient hypotheses to the reader.

Please keep your own notes about what feels right, what misrepresents your process, what it assumes, and where it asks an unnecessary question. The most useful examples include the question, the relevant response, and your preferred reasoning. You do not need to fill in a bug-report form.

Useful review cases: a lost object (your own versus someone else's); an ordinary job question; an ambiguous city; a historical chart; correcting ownership after a reading has started; asking for the exact testimony behind an answer. An answer that sounds convincing still needs your judgment. A valid evidence reference does not make an interpretation correct.

The app saves this conversation and earlier chart revisions locally. The legacy review notes are preserved. This development build is not a signed public release, and native voice and conversational quality need direct acceptance. Each launch begins a fresh reading. The small page icon in the upper right opens earlier readings in a pane. The plus icon inside it, Command–N or the native File menu opens another leaf. Saved readings reopen from that pane. Closing it returns you to the document. Touch a planet for its calculated testimony. Open “Evidence” under a passage to compare facts, chosen roles, book extracts and interpretation. Detailed diagnosis is inside the history pane's “Receipts → Processing”: original task inputs and outputs, validation, token reuse and timing. No issue form is needed.
