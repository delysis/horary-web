# Interpretation and review policy

The active reader follows the stage-specific [living method](LLM_PROCESS.md). Each task receives its own definitions, numbered procedure, contrasting examples and selected Frawley passages. The shared core's older single-response prompt remains for legacy calculator regression tests; it is not the unfolding conversation's method.

The model chooses contextual roles and proposes interpretations of supplied evidence. Native Rust owns place candidates, clock/DST conversion, chart calculation, traditional house rulers and storage. It never executes arbitrary model commands or accepts invented coordinates or ephemerides.

Intake retains the actual question and scope. Condition, directed reception and contact mechanics are independent worksheets; lost questions also receive location analysis. The final judgment combines them and appears in the main document. The app does not substitute a fixed open-outcome paragraph for every answer. Missing evidence remains unestablished, and references alone do not certify prose.

Native validation checks bounded typed fields, exact current evidence IDs, required checks, reception references, copied contact sign-change status, calculation boundaries and refusal of unsupported numeric timing. Invalid outputs and their validation errors remain recorded before a bounded repair. Valid JSON or a chart appearing does not establish a correct reading. No independent model checker or hidden reasoning transcript is claimed.

Planetary positions and hourly seven-day contacts remain approximate. Exact symbolic timing, fine event chains, intermediate stations, fixed stars and antiscia need further native work and domain assessment. No absent short-window candidate can independently answer a one-year question negatively.

Eileen's assessment should focus on question preservation, ownership and house choices, head/heart distinctions, directed and negative receptions, relevant occasion versus willingness/ability, and the contextual final answer. The prompts and exact source extracts can be inspected directly. Known readings and corrections can become annotated regression cases; current synthetic engineering tests are not her endorsement or a qualified astrology verdict.

The runtime is local. The progress journal excludes private words and coordinates, while saved reading records and private method receipts contain the exact input and original output needed for diagnosis. Detailed processing is nested inside the document's closed margins. See [privacy](PRIVACY.md), [format comparisons](FORMAT_EXPERIMENTS.md), and [verification](VERIFICATION.md).
