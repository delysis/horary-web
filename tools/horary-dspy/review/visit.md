From the supplied positions, Venus at 7.503° Scorpio is only about 0.018° from the exact trine to the Ascendant at 7.485° Pisces. Her retrograde motion carries her toward that point. This is a derived contact from e3 and e7, not a separately certified event calculation. It is the strongest positive testimony, although the tiny distance makes its precise applying/separating status sensitive to the acknowledged approximation.

Venus is in detriment and physically in the weak eighth house, despite ruling the third: Vesta is shown as having difficulty carrying out the visit, not as a bad person. She is clear of the Sun. Retrogradation could describe coming back if this is a return visit, but that circumstance is not established, so it cannot simply be counted as favorable. Jupiter has only minor dignity by term and is physically weak in the sixth, offering little additional capacity.

Venus is in Jupiter’s term: Vesta has some positive inclination toward you, although this is modest testimony. Venus is also in the Moon’s fall.
