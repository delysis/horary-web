<task>Find the actual acquisition occasion for this NEW external job. This task checks supplied contact candidates, not a hiring verdict or a calendar date.</task>

<procedure>
1. Complete relevant_actors from the accepted roles: the applicant's house ruler and applicable Moon; the external job's ruler. Direct acquisition requires an applicant–job pair (printed p.222). Do not rename an unassigned planet as the applicant, employer, rival or agent because it makes a convenient aspect.
2. Read each event's literal planets before selecting its ID. A job–unassigned-planet contact is not an applicant–job contact. A wages contact is not acquiring the job (p.223). The Sun is not a substitute job ruler for an ordinary employer; the book's exception concerns a royal appointment.
3. Complete applying_or_separating from supplied events only. Copy each selected event.withinCurrentSigns into candidate_signs. If it is false, the candidate follows an ingress: current condition/reception cannot certify that later event. A current drawing aspect without a computed application is not perfection.
4. Complete event_order by inspecting the applicant Moon's next ACTUAL aspect, not the first one that fits an offer story. Printed p.97 ordinarily uses the next aspect, or a supported next-two-aspect translation. Do not jump past earlier contacts, a Sun conjunction, station or ingress to obtain Moon–job acquisition. When the supplied later candidate has unchecked preceding contacts/sign changes, use complex_unverified: its ID can remain a conditional candidate, but it is not an established occasion. Do not explain those contacts as application steps, delays or intermediaries, and do not assume a final offer is secured. For translation/collection/prohibition, require the supplied motion, role, order and reception tests instead of inventing the mechanism.
5. Complete changing_conditions with actual supplied changes and missing tests. Lord 7 can represent rivals when the native roles establish it (p.224); an arbitrary Mars contact is not proof of a rival. A void Moon, if actually established, is a strong negative indication for required change (p.223), not something to invent from an incomplete search.
6. Complete coverage_limits precisely. Approximate hourly brackets over seven days do not certify exact perfection, every intervening station or the whole hiring process. A missing candidate is not proof of permanent rejection. Astronomical hours do not give the earthly hiring date.
7. Return basis=direct_candidate|complex_unverified|location_or_situation|no_candidate_covered, actual candidate_ids and matching candidate_signs, checks, summary and unknowns. No outcome promise or guessed date.
</procedure>

<worked_examples>
A — correct actor pair. Applicant=Jupiter and Moon; job=Venus. A Venus–Mars square is not a direct acquisition candidate unless Mars has an independently supplied relevant role. A Moon–Venus contact uses the applicant and job; a Moon–Jupiter contact joins the applicant's own significators and is not a job offer.
B — do not skip the sequence. A Moon–Venus conjunction appears only after ingress and after Moon–Mars, Moon–Saturn and Moon–Sun contacts. Correct basis: complex_unverified. Correct summary: "A later applicant–job contact is listed, but the intervening sequence and changed conditions do not establish acquisition." Wrong: "This provides a direct occasion; multiple steps/intermediaries come before the final offer is secured." Unknowns concern the unverified chain and changed reception, not merely the date of an assumed offer.
C — clean occasion. A supplied applicant–job trine is next, within current signs, with no unchecked preceding change in the supplied record. It is a relevant possible occasion. The final judgment still weighs ability and reception; trine alone is not guaranteed employment.
</worked_examples>
