<task>Answer whether the actual applicant gets this NEW external job, from the supplied evidence and completed worksheets. Give a useful plain interpretation, not a romantic psychology story or a chart glossary.</task>

<procedure>
1. Complete question_answered with the actual applicant, employer/opportunity and requested scope. An application without an offer is hoped-for acquisition, not an offer already possessed or wages paid. Preserve the original context even if the retained question is short.
2. Complete supporting_testimony by rechecking each cited fact against its actual actors. An applicant-ruler–job or applicable Moon–job occasion can support acquisition (p.222). An unassigned Mars contact, a wages contact or the applicant's own two planets meeting cannot replace it. Worksheets are proposals: correct their misreadings instead of repeating them.
3. Combine occasion, capacity and directed reception. For an ordinary job, Lord 10's minor term/face regard for the applicant is meaningful positive testimony (p.223), but still needs a relevant occasion. Weigh strong negative job→applicant testimony and the applicant's/role's actual condition. Do not let a generic "mixed" label conceal the strongest reason for your conclusion.
4. Complete contrary_testimony with genuine contrary facts. A weak house can limit the applicant's leverage; the job's poor essential condition concerns the role/company and is not proof of a specific hidden financial problem. The Moon is the applicant as worker, not a romantic heart or another person. Do not diagnose self-hatred, emotional misalignment or personal unsuitability from self-directed reception.
5. Recheck ingress and event order before relying on a later contact. Current reception cannot establish willingness at a contact after a sign change. Do not carry the Moon through a chain or past a Sun conjunction/station merely to obtain an offer. Record missing traditional tests in missing_information; do not ask the person to invent chart calculations or repeat the supplied application context.
6. Complete scope_of_answer with the actual evidence limit. A seven-day search boundary alone is not a positive or negative hiring result. Offer a cautious working conclusion if the present testimony supports one, while distinguishing unverified acquisition from definite rejection. Do not give "soon", an offer date or a calendar interval unless the timing worksheet actually establishes it.
7. Choose likely_yes, likely_no, mixed or unresolved on those reasons. Write answer in three to five short sentences: first address the job question; then the strongest useful supporting and contrary implications; then the material uncertainty. Use the employer's name where supplied. Avoid technical phrases such as "worker's heart", planet abbreviations and an unexplained software search window. Do not invent a hiring decision.
8. Return every required output field. Evidence/check references use the supplied contract, including its current array bounds; keep a short top-level evidence list and put supporting references in their appropriate checks. unknowns is an empty array if none, not ["None"].
</procedure>
