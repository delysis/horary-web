<procedure>
1. Complete question_answered with the actual applicant, employer/opportunity and requested scope. An application without an offer is hoped-for acquisition, not an offer already possessed or wages paid. Preserve the original context even if the retained question is short.
2. Complete supporting_testimony by rechecking each cited fact against its actual actors. An applicant-ruler–job or applicable Moon–job occasion can support acquisition (p.222). 3. Combine occasion, capacity and directed reception. For an ordinary job, Lord 10's minor term/face regard for the applicant is meaningful positive testimony (p.223), but still needs a relevant occasion. Preserve every quality in mixed directed rows: triplicity is moderate affinity and fall is major contrary testimony, not pure dislike or objective proof of poor job quality (pp.73,76). Weigh major negative job→applicant testimony and actual condition; "mixed" must not conceal the strongest reason for your conclusion.
4. Complete contrary_testimony with genuine contrary facts. A weak house can limit the applicant's leverage; the job's poor essential condition concerns the role/company and is not proof of a specific hidden financial problem, actual hiring requirements or a mismatch with the applicant's qualifications. Describe the unfavourable quality testimony without claiming an objectively poor personal fit from dignity alone. The Moon is the applicant as worker, not a romantic heart or another person. Do not diagnose self-hatred, emotional misalignment or personal unsuitability from self-directed reception.
5. Recheck ingress and event order before relying on a later contact. Printed p.97 does not license carrying the Moon through a long chain or a Sun conjunction/station merely to obtain an offer. Current reception cannot establish later willingness after ingress. If contacts nevertheless says "steps/intermediaries before the final offer is secured", correct that proposal: there is no established offer or intermediary, only an unchecked later candidate. Record the specific missing tests; do not ask the person to repeat already supplied application context or invent chart calculations.
6. Complete scope_of_answer with the actual evidence limit. A seven-day search boundary alone is not a positive or negative hiring result. Offer a cautious working conclusion if the present testimony supports one, while distinguishing unverified acquisition from definite rejection. If timing is unestablished, this bars negative timing claims too: "no offer in the immediate future" and "no near-term offer" invent a calendar scope just as "an offer soon" does. Say that no offer is established by the checked testimony, not when an offer will or will not happen. An offer date or calendar interval requires established timing.
7. Choose likely_yes, likely_no, mixed or unresolved on those reasons. Write answer in three to five short sentences: first address the job question; then the strongest useful supporting and contrary implications; then the material uncertainty. Use the employer's name where supplied. Avoid technical phrases such as "worker's heart", planet abbreviations and an unexplained software search window. Do not invent a hiring decision.
8. Return every required output field. Evidence/check references use the supplied contract, including its current array bounds; keep a short top-level evidence list and put supporting references in their appropriate checks. unknowns is an empty array if none, not ["None"].
</procedure>
