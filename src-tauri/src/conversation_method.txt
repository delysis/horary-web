Source: John Frawley, The Horary Textbook, 2005. References below are printed pages, not PDF positions. These are editorial paraphrases; the software's interpretation remains reviewable by Eileen.

Start with understanding the actual question and context. Cast for the moment and place the astrologer understands it, not automatically the event's date. Assign houses by the matter and ownership; use turned houses when appropriate. Traditional rulers identify significators. The querent normally has Lord 1 and the Moon, but a planet's role can differ between stages of the judgment. Do not treat keyword house suggestions as settled assignments.

Essential dignity describes condition; accidental dignity describes capacity to act. Use the printed p.72 table. Water triplicity is Mars by day and night. Bounds are exclusive upper boundaries. Negative receptions (detriment/fall) matter, and reception has direction: a guest planet in another's dignity regards that ruler. Do not silently reverse that relation or assume mutual reception is beneficial. Printed pp.44–83.

A planet within about five degrees BEFORE the next cusp may act in that next house only when in the cusp's sign; preserve the geometric position. Printed p.56. Cazimi is within 17.5 arcminutes of the Sun, combustion within 8.5 degrees, both in the same sign. Under the beams extends to 17.5 degrees. Strong angular placement is capacity, not automatic virtue or a yes.

Judge relevant applying contacts, reception and ability to bring about the matter. Distinguish applying from separating; do not restrict future contact search to drawing orbs. Check changes of sign, stations and competing contacts before declaring perfection. Translation, collection, prohibition, frustration and refranation need explicit actor and event order; software candidate events alone do not certify them. No applying contact before the Moon leaves its sign is strict void of course; an approximately fifteen-degree gap can also deserve contextual caution (printed pp.65–66). Unknown search coverage is not proof of absence.

Astronomical hours until an aspect are NOT the predicted calendar timing of an earthly event. Timing needs a relevant perfection, degree distance, plausible units from question context, sign mode, house placement and capacity/volition; do not promise hours or days without that argument. Printed timing chapter.

Lost inanimate possessions: compare Lords 2 AND 4, choose the better descriptive significator. For someone else's property use their turned second. Animals use the sixth (small) or twelfth (large). The Moon may signify the lost object applying to Lord 1 as recovery; alternatively a querent significator may apply to the object's lord. Name the roles in the specific testimony. Different roles at different stages are allowed. Prefer one main location significator, with direction/environment as possibilities, not a literal map. Debility does not automatically mean the object is damaged. Do not introduce theft unless the querent raises it. Printed pp.147–149.

For location, the chosen planet represents the OBJECT; locate it by the house it OCCUPIES. Lord 4 is an alternative object significator, not automatic evidence that the object is at home. When Lords 2 and 4 are the same planet, that alone does not establish the home as its location. Establish plausible places conversationally before translating a house into a room. The ninth can suggest a study within a home, but that conditional room meaning does not itself prove the object is at home. Fire signs suggest heat or walls; height/shelves are air-sign indications and need their own support. Printed pp.147,150–152.

Current calculation boundary: approximate inherited orbital elements and truncated lunar series; no precision certification. Hourly future samples span seven days and may miss reversals between samples. Do not invent missing lots, stars, antiscia, or event-chain tests. Explicit uncertainty is preferable to a fabricated complete reading.
